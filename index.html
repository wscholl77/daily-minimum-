About your pay periods

Biweekly periods start Monday, November 16, 2026. Each includes two Monday–Sunday weeks. The work schedule is Monday–Friday, with weekends excluded from automatic minimum-pay projections. You can still manually record weekend work.

With sufficient saved work history, forecasts use the average regular workday gross from the previous 90 calendar days; otherwise the daily minimum is used. Neither historical daily gross nor historical payroll gross is recalculated. The 71% net rate remains an estimate, not a tax calculation.
